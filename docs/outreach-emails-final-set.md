# Neighbours Club — Outreach Email Set (final, company-framed)

Standard opener across all: "I'm Alex, from Neighbours Club — a local group-buying
platform..." (company framing = more credible for B2B than "a Kanata resident
building..."). Use "we" not "I" throughout to reinforce the company. Signature
includes "Neighbours Club".

Honesty line: present as a REAL, early-stage company (true — it's deployed, part of
Scitoxe). Do NOT imply scale you don't have (no "thousands of members," no
"Kanata's leading"). Early-stage-but-real is the register.

Two hard constraints (all emails): NO launch date, NO take-rate/commission quoted.

Per-email: personalize ONE line with something specific about that business (proves
real outreach, not mass mail). Send with display name "Alex", signature to
neighborsclub.ca + phone.

Choosing which email:
- ENROLLMENT (neutral) = default broad opener, lowest-commitment, highest yes-rate,
  builds the roster/pipeline. Good for most.
- ENROLLMENT (restaurant variant) = for BIA restaurants/cafes (the 26 BIA prospects)
  — speaks to their specific pain (app fees, slow nights).
- DEAL email = for STRONG leads (wholesale-ready producers) where a near-term deal
  is plausible and you want to open a real conversation.
Best overall strategy: lead with ENROLLMENT (easy yes) -> escalate to a deal
conversation on the phone with the warm ones.

Roster honesty guardrail: "enrolled" = on the internal roster, NOT shown to members
until they have a REAL active deal (isActive:false). Keep that absolute — enrolled
never means fake-displayed to customers.

PROCESS
=====================================================================
1. Default to EMAIL 1 (or EMAIL 2 for restaurants) for broad outreach — easy yes,
   builds the roster.
2. Use EMAIL 3 for the strongest wholesale-ready leads where a deal is plausible.
3. Personalize one line per business; verify current contact info.
4. Log in tracking sheet: status (Emailed/Enrolled/Active), date, next action.
5. Enrolled-but-inactive -> nurture with EMAIL 4 on a light cadence.
6. Phone follow-up is the real validation — use merchant-outreach-research.md
   questions; test WILLINGNESS TO SHARE MARGIN for the volume you bring (that's the
   revenue-model validation). Listen for behavioral signal vs. polite "sounds cool."
