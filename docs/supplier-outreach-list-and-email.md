# Neighbours Club — Supplier Outreach List + Email (Group-Buy Validation)

Purpose: validate that group-buy has a real product in Kanata by contacting
suppliers across categories. Regional supply is fine (demand stays Kanata-local).
All leads below were found via web search on the date of this doc — VERIFY current
contact details/status before sending, and personalize each email.

Strategic note: many of these ALREADY run their own CSA/subscription/wholesale.
That means the pitch is "bring you MORE Kanata customers, we handle coordination" —
ADDITIVE, not "here's a way to sell your stuff" (they've solved that). Their
reaction is itself validation: additive value vs. redundant.

Weighting: lead with suppliers that ALREADY have wholesale/partnership programs
(roasters, wholesale bakeries) — they're actively seeking channels, so likeliest
to see you as additive. Farms with their own Kanata CSA may see you as redundant
(still worth contacting — the reaction is data).

THE EMAIL (revised — "additive value" framing for established producers)

Subject: Bringing more Kanata customers to [business] — a quick question

Hi [Name],

I'm Alex, a Kanata resident building a local group-buying platform — a way for
Kanata neighbourhoods to pool orders and buy together from great local producers
like you.

I know you already have your own [CSA / subscription / wholesale], so I'll be
direct: I'm not trying to replace what works for you. I'm building a way to bring
you MORE Kanata customers — neighbours who order together as a batch, delivered to
one local pickup point — without you having to do the coordination.

It's early days, and I'm talking to a few local producers to understand what would
actually be useful. Could I call you for 10 minutes this week to learn how your
[product] works and whether reaching more Kanata neighbourhoods would interest you?

Thanks,
Alex
[phone] · neighborsclub.ca
